My daughter and son have such different communication styles that it's like they're from different planets. One constantly checks in, while the other... well, let me know if you spot him.

With my daughter, we text and FaceTime multiple times a day. She calls just to say a quick hello. I rarely initiate FaceTime with her because I want to respect her space, but she reaches out consistently.

My son? I literally have to say, "Are you alive?" to which he will always reply, “No.” Days can go by without communication. He doesn't respond to Instagram reels I send, though he'll occasionally send me some.

However, if HE initiates the call, we might talk for 30-40 minutes straight. What has been interesting is that in the past he would call for one of three reasons: he needs money, he needs help with something, or (rarely) he just feels like talking. Lately, it has changed; he is calling to tell me about his life. Sometimes it is to talk about diving school or some show he’s watched or to tell me what he has made for dinner. But sometimes it is to have a real conversation like two adults; most recently it has been to think through next steps in his professional life. I treasure these conversations.

But then the next call is usually to ask me to DoorDash him something for dinner.

I'm growing comfortable with these radically different communication styles. But I still check his location when I haven't heard from him in a while – nt in a field or the ocean? Great, probably fine then!

Parents with older kids, can you relate? And if you have sons and daughters, do you notice similar communication patterns?

When people ask me about creating real change in organizations, I share this truth: Focus on what you can actually influence.

As popularized by [Stephen M. R. Covey](https://www.linkedin.com/in/stephen-m-r-covey-6400191a5), there are three spheres we need to think about:

1️⃣ What we have immediate control over

2️⃣What we have influence over

3️⃣ What we have concern about but can't directly impact

Here's the thing: the world is messy and overwhelming. Always has been, always will be.

But I've learned that real progress happens when we stop hand-wringing about everything that's broken and start focusing on those first two spheres: what we can control and influence.

Yes, the challenges can feel overwhelming. But change happens one small step at time. Instead of getting paralyzed by everything that needs fixing, ask yourself: "What can I actually do right now, where I am?"

That's not just wishful thinking – that's how real transformation begins.

Threshold attributes© might be the key to less biased hiring. Let me explain.

Threshold attributes are the behaviors and elements that make people successful in your organization. Take integrity or intellectual curiosity: These aren't things you can learn in a classroom– they're behaviors you demonstrate.

But how someone developed these attributes doesn't matter. What matters is how they show up in their actions.

For example, a candidate might've been a doorman and still perfectly demonstrate the intellectual curiosity your organization needs. It's not about where they got the attribute, but about how they use it.

Yes, hiring according to threshold attributes requires more work from organizations. You need to:

→ Define your values and key attributes.

→ Identify what behaviors demonstrate them.

→ Understand how they vary across roles and seniority levels.

But the result? A clearer, less biased way to build your team.

This isn't just another HR framework – it's about fundamentally rethinking how we evaluate talent

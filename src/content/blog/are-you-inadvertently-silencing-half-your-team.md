Are you inadvertently silencing half your team? Here's a quick audit:

❓Do you always send agendas before meetings and follow up with notes afterward?

If not, you might be missing valuable input from introverts and those who need processing time before responding.

When we fail to create these structures, we typically only hear from people like me – folks who can immediately give an answer or opinion (and trust me, I'm very good at giving opinions!).

Inclusive leadership isn't just about big DEI initiatives. It's about practical mechanisms that ensure everyone can contribute fully:

☑️ Sending agendas in advance

☑️ Providing follow-up notes

☑️ Creating consistent opportunities for sharing

☑️ Making space for different communication styles

These simple practices aren't just "nice to have" – they're foundational to building cultures where diverse perspectives actually get heard.

The result? Better decisions, increased innovation, and a workplace where people feel valued for their unique contributions AND connected to something larger than themselves.

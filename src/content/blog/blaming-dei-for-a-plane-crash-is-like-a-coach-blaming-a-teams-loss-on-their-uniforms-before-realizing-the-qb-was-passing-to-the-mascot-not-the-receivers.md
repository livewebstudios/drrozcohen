Blaming DEI for a plane crash is like a coach blaming a team’s loss on their uniforms before realizing the QB was passing to the mascot not the receivers. Read: Trump's press conference was a shitshow.

We knew it was coming. But the speed and immediacy of recent executive orders – placing all federal DEI and accessibility employees on leave, canceling trainings, removing websites – still hits hard.

Yes, organizations like the ACLU and Southern Poverty Law Center will take this to court. But I'm thinking about the immediate human impact.

I'm thinking about:

- The FAA employees maligned by false claims about hiring practices that misrepresent both the agency and people with disabilities
- The dedicated professionals suddenly forced on administrative leave
- The individuals who fall under the "DEI umbrella" being told they don't matter
- The message this sends about who belongs and who doesn't across our institutions.

People keep saying "stay joyful" and "keep fighting." And yes, we must.\
But let's be honest – the anxiety and sense of doom isn't easy to shake.

I don't have neat, tidy answers today. What I do know is that I'll continue doing this work – creating cultures of belonging where everyone's humanity is recognized and valued. Not because it's easy, but because it's necessary.

This moment is overwhelming. But we’re moving forward anyway.

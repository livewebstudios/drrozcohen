I want to call out two examples of leadership behavior from the past few weeks.

First, did you see Trudeau's response to Trump’s tariffs?

What I liked about Trudeau was how he spoke directly to the people of the US. He recognized that the voice of one person – even though that person was elected by some of us – isn't representative of how all Americans feel or think. He stood up and advocated for his country and people the way leaders should.

(And yes, the fact that he called out Trump as…let’s just say “less than intelligent”.. was not the worst thing about his speech.)

Then let’s talk about how Zelensky handled himself in the Oval Office meeting where he was berated and taken to task (when clearly there was an ulterior motive).

In both situations, these leaders exemplified inclusive and authentic leadership behaviors. They were transparent in their communication, talking openly about challenges and opportunities, even when dealing with these complex diplomatic situations.

They remained consistent in their values, maintaining integrity and consistent principles in challenging relationships.

They demonstrated vulnerability by Showing human qualities and acknowledging limitations while maintaining necessary diplomatic strength.

That's leadership. That's what Trump is NOT doing.

These past couple of weeks continue to make me wonder how we will all come through this as individuals and as a nation.

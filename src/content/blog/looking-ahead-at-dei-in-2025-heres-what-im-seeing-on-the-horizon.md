Looking ahead at DEI in 2025, here's what I'm seeing on the horizon.

→ First, we'll continue to see DEI under attack, particularly with the new administration and increased polarization.

What's interesting, though, is how different this feels from previous election cycles. Last time, we saw many marginalized individuals and underrepresented groups taking an activist stance.

Now, I'm noticing something different: people are tired. Those who have been seen as "less than" because of their identity are exhausted from constantly fighting for basic respect and dignity.

→ What needs to happen next? Those with privilege and voice need to step up as advocates and allies.

It's time to move beyond performative allyship to taking real stands against injustice when we see it. Call out people, comments, systems, and foundations that work against inclusion and connection. I call upon [Simon Sinek](https://www.linkedin.com/in/simonsinek) and his influential work as a way to ask a simple question: “Why?”

→ I also predict we'll see a deepening divide – but I want to be precise here: this isn't about Democrats versus Republicans. This is about ideologies and how they impact our workplaces and communities.

Some organizations will retreat. Others, like Costco and Apple, will double down on their commitment to building inclusive cultures.

💡But through it all the real question will be: which side of history will most choose?

What are your predictions for DEI in 2025? I'd love to hear your thoughts.

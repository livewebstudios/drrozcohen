Employee engagement is one of the key indicators in gauging work satisfaction. In today’s society, employees want to be involved in their work, enthusiastic about their company, and have a sense of belonging among their team. In fact, 79% of respondents in a [Global Human Capital survey](https://www2.deloitte.com/us/en/insights/focus/human-capital-trends/2020/creating-a-culture-of-belonging.html) mentioned that fostering a sense of belonging in the workforce was essential to their organization’s success in the next 12-18 months.

## Employee Engagement Is Important for All Companies

Employee engagement is all about improving the work environment and culture to encourage employees to be dedicated to their company’s goals and values. Having effective strategies in place helps create a better work culture, increase productivity, reduce staff turnover, and even impact company profits. More importantly than any of these, it makes employees happy and turns them into your best advocates.

## Why Employee Engagement Matters to Workers

Being engaged in your job, company, and colleagues plays a critical role in overall satisfaction. You’ll not only be more energized and efficient but go beyond what’s expected of you. This can influence those around you, including your colleagues and customers.

## Why Employee Engagement Matters to Companies

Employee engagement matters at a company level because of the impact it has on overall business operations and profitability. Additionally, it also helps leaders understand employee needs and identify new ways to improve morale.

## Benefits of Employee Engagement for Startups

There are several benefits of investing in employee engagement for your startup. These include:

-   **Improved employee loyalty:** Focusing on employee engagement will help ensure your employees stick around longer. It can help keep employee retention high and minimize employee turnover costs. If you decide not to invest in employee engagement, it can cause low engagement and disinterest among your employees, who will be more likely to jump ship quickly and often.

-   **Increase productivity:** Studies show that engaged employees work harder because they like what they do and believe in their value to the company. If you are interested in something and feel connected to your coworkers and company, it’s more likely you will be invested in the long run.

-   **Better Customer Service:** Employee engagement leads to better customer service and client success, which are both crucial to any successful business. Happy and engaged workers are more likely to care and go above and beyond, which can lead to client retention, securing upsells, and getting more customer referrals.

How do we create connected workplaces where people truly belong? One bite at a time.

As leaders, we're often tempted to believe we need to have all the answers. We don't.

Instead of prescribing solutions, try this:

💡 Sit down with your team and say, "I want to create a space where people feel more connected and belong to both our team and our organization. What would you suggest we do?"

This simple approach accomplishes two things:

1. It gives people a chance to share their ideas (people support what they help create).
2. It demonstrates you're genuinely interested in hearing what others think.

People know what they need to feel connected – they just need the opportunity to share it.

Creating cultures of belonging isn't about grand gestures. It's about consistency, respect, and intellectual curiosity. It's about providing space for people to share pieces of themselves they normally wouldn't, which opens doors for connections that might not otherwise happen.

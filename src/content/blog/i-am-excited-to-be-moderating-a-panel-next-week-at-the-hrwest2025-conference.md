Friends and Colleagues, I am excited to be moderating a panel next week at the #HRWest2025 Conference. I have the privilege of talking with [Amy Madden, SHRM-SCP](https://www.linkedin.com/in/amy-madden-shrm-scp-0401b058), [Sarah Walker](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sarahwalks), and [Selena Yuan (She/Her)](https://www.linkedin.com/in/selenayuan)  about how to align people and purpose for outstanding performance. Specifically, we will discuss strategies in organizations that cultivate a culture of adaptability and resilience with plenty of time to answer questions.

If you're attending, make sure to stop by and say hi! And if you want to attend, let me know, and I'll share a discount code for a free conference pass!

I’ll be direct: The constant barrage of headlines, executive orders, and policy changes is exhausting. And that’s exactly what it’s designed to do.

There’s a phenomenon called “learned helplessness” - that feeling that so much is coming at you, and there’s absolutely nothing you can do about it. I believe there’s an intentionality behind the current administration’s approach to create exactly this response in all of us.

So how am I fighting this? Here’s my approach:

- I strictly limit my news intake to 15-20 minutes per day from reputable sources
- I set firm boundaries - no political conversations after 8pm (it just keeps me awake)
- I focus on making practical impacts where I can (like choosing which companies get my business based on their values)
- I remind myself daily that feeling overwhelmed is the intended outcome - and refuse to give in to it

The sense of doom and anxiety isn’t easy to shake. But when we retreat and do nothing, that’s exactly what those who want to dismantle progress are hoping for.

This isn’t about politics. It’s about maintaining your capacity to continue doing meaningful work when everything seems designed to drain your energy.

How are you protecting your mental space while staying engaged? I’d love to hear your strategies.

Costco's stance on inclusion is clear: their workforce should represent the communities they serve. That's not just nice messaging – it's smart business.

Let's be honest about why this matters.

Here's what the research consistently shows: when you have a variety of perspectives involved in decision-making and problem-solving, you get better results. Period.

Different ideologies, different approaches, different ways of thinking… they all lead to stronger solutions (see anything written by Scott E. Page, Ruchika T. Malhotra, Jennifer Brown or Lily Zheng).

This isn't just about doing good (though that matters). It's about doing better business. When organizations truly embrace diverse perspectives:

↳ Problems get solved more effectively.

↳ Innovation increases.

↳ Solutions become more sustainable.

↳ Financial outcomes improve.

The real question organizations need to ask themselves: Why are you doing this work? Is it about checking a box? Or is it about fundamental business success?

Your answer will determine whether your efforts create lasting change or just temporary noise.

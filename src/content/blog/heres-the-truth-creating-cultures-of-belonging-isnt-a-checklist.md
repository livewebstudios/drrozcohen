Here's the truth: Creating cultures of belonging isn't a checklist. If your company is looking for a "10 Step Plan to Diversity," I'm afraid you're going to be disappointed.

This work is deeply contextual. The approach has to align with:

- Why your organization cares about this work
- Your current organizational reality
- The unique needs of your people
- Your industry and company culture
- Where you're starting from and where you want to go

Is the work driven by shareholder pressure? Leadership conviction? A response to internal conflict? The answer changes everything about your approach.

You can't take an organization that hasn't done any work in this space and suddenly transform it into a fully realized inclusive culture. You have to meet them where they are and take it step by step.

That's why I focus on asking the right questions rather than providing cookie-cutter solutions. Questions like:

- Why is this process the way it is?
- Who does it affect?
- Is it having the impact you intend?

These questions uncover the why behind the what – the foundation for meaningful change.

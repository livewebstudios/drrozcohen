Excited to announce I'm moderating a panel at HRWest Conference this March. It’s called "AI: Empowering Innovation While Protecting the Human Element."

Here's why I'm excited about this discussion:

We're not just talking about whether to use AI in HR (that ship has sailed). We're diving into the real questions:

🫶 How do we harness AI without losing the human touch that makes HR... well, human?

🫶 What questions should we ask of ourselves and each other before implementing AI tools?

🫶 How do we protect equity, privacy, and authentic connection in an AI-enhanced workplace?

Let's be real: AI promises to streamline everything from recruiting to talent management. But at what cost to the relationships we build? To the cultures we nurture?

This panel is about implementing AI thoughtfully so it enhances rather than diminishes the human element of HR.

Join me March 11-12 at HRWest as we unpack:

- A balanced view of AI's impact on core HR processes
- Critical questions for evaluating AI tools in your organization
- Practical strategies to preserve human connection in an AI world

Who else will I see there?

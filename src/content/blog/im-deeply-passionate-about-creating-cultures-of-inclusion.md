I recently had a wonderful conversation with Dr. Jonathan Westover on the Human Capital Leadership podcast about something I'm deeply passionate about: creating cultures of inclusion.

We explored how finding commonalities can break down barriers and build bridges – even in today's polarized environment.

I shared insights about how simple, consistent practices can create spaces where people feel comfortable sharing who they truly are.

Some key topics we covered:

- Why connection matters more than ever in today's workplace

- How leaders can create space for authentic sharing

- Simple practices that build cultures of belonging

- Why focusing on what we can influence creates real change

Want to hear more about building radically connected cultures?

Check out the full episode at the link in the comments. Thank you again, [Jonathan H. Westover, PhD, MPA, AF-CIPD, SFHEA](https://www.linkedin.com/in/jonathanhwestover), for having me on the show! It was such a meaningful conversation about work that matters.

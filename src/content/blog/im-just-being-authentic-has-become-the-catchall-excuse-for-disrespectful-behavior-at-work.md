"I'm just being authentic" has become the catchall excuse for disrespectful behavior at work.

Let's be clear: authenticity doesn't mean being blunt or in-your-face. It's not about telling someone "I hate your background" and claiming you're just showing who you are.

Here's what's fascinating: you can be an incredibly authentic leader – meaning your behavior matches your words (see President Trump) – and still not be effective for your organization or the people you're leading (also see President Trump).

Authenticity alone isn't enough. What matters is:

↳ Whether you have people's best interests in mind

↳ How you're moving the organization forward

↳ Whether you're creating an inclusive environment

↳ How you're helping others succeed

Being your authentic self at work is essential. But authenticity without consideration for others isn't leadership – it's just behavior that doesn't serve your people or your organization.

What does authentic leadership mean to you?
